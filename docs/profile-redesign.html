Kria player app · You tab · Design proposal · 09 Oct 2026

Your profile as a player card

Today's profile is one long column: your email under your name, a lone Events count, a table, then knockouts, an orange quick-matches card, honours and two menus. This version reads like a broadcast player card. Who you are and your numbers come first, then how you play each sport, what you have won, and your history. Account settings go to the bottom. It stays inside the Kria design system, the same one the new home uses.

What isn't working today

Header

Your email is the headline

The line under your name is your email address, in capitals. It is private, and it says nothing about you as a player.

Stats

A lone zero

"Events 0" fills a full-width strip of its own and is the largest number on the first screen.

Career

Nothing leads

The best-sport card waits for 10 decided matches. Until then the first thing you read is a table.

Structure

One long stack

Eleven blocks in one column. Cancelled knockouts sit beside finished ones, and the orange Quick matches card is narrower than everything around it.

Consistency

Two styles for one result

Results here use outlined W/L boxes; home uses solid ones. The design system says status is a solid fill.

Achievements

Hidden from you

Other players see your milestone achievements on your public profile. Your own profile never shows them.

What building it takes

  • Player cardA rewrite of the top of profile.tsx. The figures come from data the screen already loads: your career profile, honours and event count.
  • Sport cardsA new component in place of the career table on this screen.
  • Trophy cabinetBrings together the honour badges and the milestone data that already exist (HonorsList, Achievements).
  • History switchMatches shares one result row with home, so the two screens can never drift apart. Knockouts reuses KnockoutRow; Teams reuses PlayedForCard.
  • MenuQuick matches added to Playing in profileMenu.ts.
  • ServerNothing. Everything shown is already loaded today.

Decide before I build

  1. Other players' profiles too?"Seen by others" shows the same layout without your account section. Building both at once keeps them consistent.
  2. Email off the profile?It moves to Edit profile, and nothing else on the page shows it.
  3. History as one switch?A switch keeps the page short. The alternative is three short lists stacked, with everything visible at once.
