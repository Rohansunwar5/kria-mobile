Kria player app · Home screen · Design proposal · 09 Oct 2026

Home, in matchday order

Today's home stacks six blocks of equal weight and opens on a Host card the nav already covers. This version orders home by what needs you first: your own game, then what is live, then what you can enter, then how you have been playing. Everything stays inside the existing Kria design system: Anton, Space Grotesk and Space Mono, hard 6px edges, solid status fills.

What isn't working today

Top of screen

It opens on a duplicate

The large "Start a match" card repeats the pink Host button in the nav, and it takes most of the first screen.

Hierarchy

Nothing leads

Six sections share one heading size and one card style, so the eye has no first place to land.

Your live match

The urgent thing is buried

A match you are playing right now sits inside Recent matches, below your record and the tournament row.

Results

A card per match

Every past match is its own bordered card, so two results fill the space five could use.

The proposal

Most visits. Your player card leads, with the join code built in.

What building it takes

  • Your game heroOne new component with three states, fed by data home already loads: your career profile and your quick matches. HomeHero.tsx
  • Live ticker chipA compact chip for home only. The full Live screen keeps its current rows.
  • Tournament posterGrows the existing tile, adds the data strip and a deadline countdown from registrationDeadline.
  • Results and top players ledgerRestyles the existing rows into one panel. "All" goes to the existing /matches/[playerId] screen.
  • ServerNothing planned. I'll confirm the quick-match list and the tournament list already return game scores and the deadline before building those two pieces.

Decide before I build

  1. A date line on the player card?You removed the header. The card carries a small "Your game · Fri 09 Oct" line. Keep it or drop it?
  2. Host only in the nav?Between matches, home offers Join only. Hosting is the pink nav button. New accounts still get both.
  3. Top players on home?It is the least personal section. It could stay, or move to Explore to keep home shorter.
