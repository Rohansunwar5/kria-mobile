Empty means "here's how", never "nothing here"

Every slot that would hold a number shows the shortest real path to filling it. That's 10 decided matches for a rank, 2 sports for the nearest milestone, and a code from a friend for the first match. No fake data, no sample charts.

The profile gap is a real blocker

Tournament registration copies age and gender from the profile, so a player without a date of birth or gender can't enter. The checklist on Home and Profile is the same one. Tap its items to fill them in.
