The score is the screen.

How a live match reads, at three depths: a feed row, a sport-specific scoreboard, then detail on demand. Players watch; only staff score, and that stays in the organiser portal.

One rule per sport

Badminton: the current game's points are the hero. Finished games sit beside them as small numbers, and game point or match point appear only when they're true.

Cricket: the chase is the hero ("need 32 off 22"), worked out from target and overs in cricketLiveState. Batters, the bowler and this over sit underneath.

Data and motion

The feed is GET /live-feed: tournament or quick, title, scoreline, tournament, category and round. Quick rows never show a join code. Match screens join the socket room match:{id} and update in place. The only motion is a quick change on the number that moved.
