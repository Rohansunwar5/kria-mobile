KRIA · MOTION

The ball is the brand. Let it move.

The mark's head is a ball, so every brand motion starts there. The ball drops, gets thrown or orbits, and the runner meets it. Everything below is live: launches loop, and every tile has a trigger.

Rules

The launch plays once per cold start and is cut short the moment Home is ready. Brand motion stays on launch and loading. Everything else is functional, 100–320ms, with no loops except loaders. Reduced motion swaps every movement for a fade (try the Tweak).

Production notes

The logo provided is an 80×70 PNG, so it looks soft at launch size. Ship an SVG with the ball as its own layer and build these in Lottie or Reanimated. "KRIA" is set in Archivo as a stand-in until there's a real wordmark.
