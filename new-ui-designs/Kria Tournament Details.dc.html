One tournament, from sign-up to final.

Arohance Badminton Tournament followed through its whole lifecycle. Each phone is one stage the player sees. Use the Stage tweak to see 2a adapt to each point in the lifecycle. Pairs with the player app in Kria Player App.

What the backend gives players (public reads)

Tournament, categories, teams + rosters, the bracket (matches by category, linked by nextMatchId), the category leaderboard, auction status + sold log (polled), and announcements (pinned first). Each category runs its own lifecycle: registration → auction → groups → bracket → ongoing → completed.

Left out on purpose

Organiser and staff actions (approve, bid entry, scoring, reshuffle) stay in the organiser portal. Registration counts are organiser-only analytics, so players see the cap ("limited to 64"), never "41 joined". In a manual auction, bids are called out at the venue and entered by staff, so players watch the auction but can't bid from the app.
