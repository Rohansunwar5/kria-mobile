KRIA · PLAYER APP · DIRECTION 1

Your sporting career, in your pocket.

Eight key screens, each tappable. Links inside a phone jump to the screen they open. Data is realistic mock data shaped exactly like the kria-server responses.

Built to what the backend actually does

Rankings are win rate, not points. rankBySport orders by won ÷ decided, only for players with 10+ decided matches, top 50, per sport. Every ranking screen says so. No rank movement is shown because none is stored.

Quick matches are join-by-code. No venue, time or public feed exists, so Compete › Matches is "your matches + join with code", not a map of games.

Reference patterns, adapted

Status-first home (fitness summary apps): one number answers "where do I stand", then one action.

Editorial ranked rows + pinned self (segment leaderboards): no podium, your row always reachable.

Minted medals (run-club trophy cases): one coin language, tier carried by the rim metal only.
